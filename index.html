Scope Boundaries

What the engagement covers, and what falls outside it.

In scope

  • Technical SEO design and implementation support
  • Information architecture, URL and redirect plan
  • Metadata, internal linking and structured data specifications
  • SEO, AEO and GEO content system for the priority page set
  • Crawler, sitemap and AI access controls
  • Entity and external evidence alignment
  • Search and AI measurement framework
  • Training, launch support and the improvement backlog

Out of scope

  • Guaranteed rankings or AI citations — no engine permits this
  • Paid search and paid social media
  • Mass AI content production and scaled question pages
  • Fabricated reviews, mentions or purchased links
  • Unapproved health, spiritual or performance claims
  • Website rebuild and platform migration — quoted separately
  • Marketplace and quick commerce listing operations
  • Ongoing monthly content volume beyond the launch set
