Executive recommendation

Three views of the same discovery system

01

SEO indexes and ranks

Technical access makes the site crawlable, so the pages customers search for can actually be found and ranked.

02

AEO answers and qualifies

Structured product and educational content makes Darshan the clearest source for a direct answer, then carries that reader onward.

03

GEO earns the citation

Consistent brand evidence gives Google, Gemini and ChatGPT enough confidence to cite or recommend the brand by name.

Why it holds

Google states its generative features run on core Search ranking and quality systems, so the programme prioritises durable search quality rather than short-lived AI tactics.

Technical access, then meaning, then evidence, then experience. Each layer only works if the one before it is in place.
